The story

In 208 AD, after his crushing defeat at the Battle of Red Cliffs (赤壁), the Wei warlord Cao Cao fled through the narrow Huarong Pass — where the Shu commander Guan Yu blocked his way. Remembering an old debt of gratitude, Guan Yu let him go.

On the board, the big 2×2 block is Cao Cao; the horizontal block below him is Guan Yu; the four tall blocks are the generals 张飞 赵云 马超 黄忠; the small blocks are soldiers. Slide the blocks — none may be lifted — until Cao Cao reaches the opening at the bottom.

Professor Jiang Changying described the puzzle in his 1949 book Science Entertainment and named it 华容道. A nearly identical puzzle was patented in England by J. H. Fleming in 1932, and the 81-move minimum for the classic layout was worked out by Thomas B. Lemann (Scientific American, March 1964).
